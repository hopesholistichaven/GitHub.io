ABOUT EILEEN LANG

Eileen Lang is the Founder of Hope's Holistic Haven, a wellness-centered organization dedicated to holistic health, healing, self-discovery, and personal transformation. Named in honor of her late father, Hope's Holistic Haven reflects Eileen's commitment to carrying forward the wisdom, values, and traditions passed down through generations while building a legacy of empowerment and compassionate care.

Born in Brooklyn, New York, and deeply connected to her Caribbean heritage, Eileen is a fourth-generation Afro-Caribbean Spiritualist and multidisciplinary practitioner. Her diverse educational and professional background spans Culinary Arts, Holistic Health and Nutrition, Clinical Hypnotherapy, Thanatology, and Integrative Medicine and Health Sciences, allowing her to approach wellness through a comprehensive and integrative lens.

Eileen combines evidence-informed holistic practices with ancestral wisdom and intuitive guidance, incorporating modalities such as Storytelling, Energy Readings, Cardology, Lenormand, Astrology, just to name a few. Through her work, she helps individuals cultivate self-awareness, personal growth, and a deeper connection to themselves.

Guided by a passion for healing, education, and transformation, Eileen's mission is to empower others to live with greater purpose, balance, and fulfillment while honoring the legacy of those who came before them.
