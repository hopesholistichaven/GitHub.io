Eileen Lang

Eileen Lang is the Founder of Hope’s Holistic Haven, a practitioner, mentor, and compassionate guide for those navigating life transitions.

Born in Brooklyn, New York, she developed a strong connection to service, spirituality, and community from an early age.

Eileen combines evidence-informed practices with intuitive guidance to support clients in grief, stress, and emotional healing.

Guided by a passion for healing and a belief in the body’s ability to restore itself, Eileen creates spaces that feel deeply supportive and empowering.
